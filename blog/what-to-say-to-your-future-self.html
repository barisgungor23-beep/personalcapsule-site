Future Self Letters

What to Say to Your Future Self (When You Don't Know Where to Start)

PersonalCapsule · 4 min read

The blank page can be intimidating. If you've ever wondered what to say to your future self, the answer is simpler than it feels: say what's true for you right now. Here are honest topics and example lines to help you start.

Start with where you are

Future you will have forgotten the small, ordinary details — and those are often the most moving to reread.

Example lines:

  • "Right now I'm sitting in…"
  • "These days I keep thinking about…"
  • "A normal week for me looks like…"

Say what you're feeling

You don't have to be wise or polished. Naming your current mood honestly is enough.

Example lines:

  • "Honestly, I feel…"
  • "I'm proud of myself for…"
  • "I'm struggling with…"

Name a hope and a worry

This is the part future you will be most curious about.

Example lines:

  • "I'm hoping that by now you've…"
  • "I'm a little afraid that…"
  • "I really don't want to lose…"

Capture your reasoning

If you're in the middle of a decision, write down why you're choosing what you're choosing. Later, this protects you from rewriting the story in hindsight.

Example lines:

  • "I'm choosing this because…"
  • "The options I considered were…"
  • "I expect this will…"

Ask questions

Questions turn a letter into a conversation across time.

A few to borrow:

  • "Did the thing I was worried about actually matter?"
  • "What do you wish I'd done differently?"
  • "Are you happier now?"

What you don't need to say

You don't need predictions, big lessons, or perfect grammar. You don't need to sound like anyone but yourself. A short, honest note is more valuable than an impressive one.
