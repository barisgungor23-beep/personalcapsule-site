Future Self Letters

Letter to Your Future Self Examples

PersonalCapsule · 7 min read

A letter to your future self can be hard to start. Examples help because they show what the letter can sound like. It does not need to be perfect, poetic, or long. It only needs to feel honest.

You can write to yourself one year from now, five years from now, after a big change, on your birthday, or at a moment when you may need encouragement.

Use these letter to your future self examples as starting points. Copy the structure if it helps, but change the details so the letter sounds like you.

How to use these future self letter examples

Do not copy an example word for word unless it truly fits you. The best future self letter includes your own memories, goals, fears, questions, and small details from today.

  • Choose the example that matches your situation
  • Keep the opening if it helps you begin
  • Replace general lines with details from your life
  • Add one thing you hope future you remembers
  • Choose when you want to open the letter
  • Save it somewhere private

A simple sample letter can become meaningful when you make it specific.

Dear future me letter example

This example works well if you want a warm, simple letter that feels personal without being too long.

Dear future me,

I hope you are reading this at a time when life feels a little clearer.

Right now, I am still figuring things out. Some days I feel excited. Some days I feel unsure. I do not know exactly where we will be when you open this, but I hope you remember that I kept trying.

I hope you are proud of the small steps. I hope you did not forget why this mattered. I hope you are kinder to yourself than I sometimes am right now.

If things are hard when you read this, take a breath. You have already made it through days you once thought would be too much.

With love,
Past me

Letter to your future self example for one year

A one-year future self letter is good when you want to check in with your goals, habits, and daily life. One year is close enough to feel real but far enough to notice change.

Dear future me,

One year from now, I hope you can see how much has changed.

Today, life feels busy and a little uncertain. I am trying to be more patient with myself. I am trying to build habits that actually support the life I want, not just the life I think I should want.

I hope you kept going with the things that mattered. I hope you let go of at least one thing that was making you heavy. I hope you found more peace in your normal days.

Please remember this version of us. The one who was trying, even without proof that everything would work out.

See you in one year,
Past me

Letter to your future self example for five years

A five-year letter can be deeper. It is useful for big questions about identity, relationships, work, and the kind of life you want to build.

Dear future me,

Five years feels far away from here.

I wonder what became important to you. I wonder what stopped mattering. I wonder who stayed close, what changed, and what surprised you most.

Right now, I am hoping to build a life that feels honest. I do not want to choose things only because they look good from the outside. I want to choose what feels true, even if it takes longer.

If you are reading this and life is not what I expected, I hope you are still proud of the path. I hope you chose with care. I hope you learned how to trust yourself.

Please do not forget the person who wrote this. They were doing their best with what they knew.

With love,
Past me

Letter to future self example before a big change

This example works before a move, graduation, breakup, new job, new school, or any season that feels uncertain.

Dear future me,

I am writing this before everything changes.

Part of me is excited. Part of me is scared. I keep wondering if I am ready, but maybe readiness is not the point. Maybe this is one of those moments where I have to move forward before I feel completely certain.

I hope you remember what this felt like. I hope you remember the courage it took to begin. If the change was hard, I hope it still taught you something. If it turned out better than expected, I hope you let yourself enjoy it.

Whatever happened, I hope you know this: I was brave enough to step into the unknown.

Past me

Short letter to your future self example

A future self letter can be only a few sentences. Short letters are useful when you want to save one clear feeling or reminder.

Dear future me,

I hope you remember why you started.

Right now, I am trying to trust the process, even when it feels slow. If you are reading this later, I hope you can see that the small steps mattered.

Keep going.
Past me

Emotional letter to your future self example

This example is for a moment when you want to be honest with future you about something tender or difficult.

Dear future me,

I do not know what life feels like for you right now, but I hope it is softer than it feels today.

I am carrying a lot. Some of it is visible. Some of it is not. I am trying to keep going without becoming hard. I am trying to believe that this season will not last forever.

If you are reading this from a better place, please remember how much strength it took to get there. If things are still hard, please do not be cruel to yourself. You are not behind. You are living.

I hope you found moments of light. I hope you let people love you. I hope you became someone who feels safe to be.

With care,
Past me

Letter to future self template

If you want to write your own letter, use this simple future self letter template.

Dear future me,

Right now, I am…

The thing I hope you remember is…

One thing I am working on is…

I am proud of…

I am worried about…

A question I want to ask you is…

If life feels different now, I hope…

Please do not forget…

With love,
Past me

You can keep the template short or turn each line into a full paragraph.

How to start a letter to your future self

If the first line feels difficult, use a simple opening. You can always make it more personal later.

  • Dear future me,
  • If you are reading this, I hope you remember...
  • Right now, I am...
  • I am writing this because...
  • Today feels important because...
  • One thing I do not want you to forget is...
  • I hope you are proud of...
  • If life feels hard when you read this, I want you to know...

The opening does not need to be impressive. It only needs to help you begin.

What to write in a letter to your future self

A future self letter can include anything you may want to remember later. If you feel stuck, choose a few simple topics.

  • What your life looks like right now
  • What you are working toward
  • What you are afraid of
  • What you are proud of
  • What decision you are facing
  • Who matters to you
  • What you hope changes
  • What you hope stays the same
  • What you want to forgive yourself for
  • What future you should remember first

The more specific you are, the more powerful the letter will feel later.

FAQ

What is a good letter to your future self example?
A good letter to your future self example describes your life now, what you hope to remember, what you are working toward, and what you want future you to know. It should sound honest, not perfect.
How do I start a letter to my future self?
Start with a simple line like “Dear future me,” “Right now, I am...” or “If you are reading this, I hope you remember...” The first sentence only needs to help you begin.
Can I use a future self letter template?
Yes. A template can help you start. Use it as a structure, then add your own details, memories, goals, and questions.
How long should a future self letter be?
It can be a few sentences or several pages. A short, specific letter is often more meaningful than a long letter that feels forced.
What should I write in a letter to my future self?
Write about your current life, hopes, fears, goals, decisions, relationships, and anything you want future you to remember.
When should I open a future self letter?
Common open dates include 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, 10 years, a birthday, graduation, New Year, or a major life milestone.
Can I send a letter to my future self by email?
Yes. Email works well for plain text. If you want photos, voice notes, a sealed open date, or a more private capsule experience, an app may be a better fit.
Can I save a future self letter privately?
