Open When You Miss Me: A Letter Idea for Long Distance Love

PersonalCapsule · 4 min read

An open when you miss me letter is one of the most emotional open when letters you can write. It is especially meaningful for long-distance relationships, travel, college, deployment, moving away, or any season where two people who love each other cannot be together every day.

The purpose is not to erase the distance. The purpose is to make the person feel close to you for a few minutes.

Why this letter matters

Missing someone can feel quiet and heavy. It often happens during ordinary moments: before sleep, during a familiar song, after a long day, or when something reminds them of you.

An open when you miss me letter gives your partner something real to hold, read, or save when that feeling arrives.

It says:

“I knew this moment might come, and I wanted to be there somehow.”

What to include in an open when you miss me letter

A strong letter should include four things:

1. Acknowledge the feeling

Start by naming why they opened it.

Example:

“You opened this because you miss me. I wish I could be there right now.”

2. Say you miss them too

This matters. It makes the feeling mutual, not one-sided.

Example:

“I miss you too. I miss your voice, your laugh, and the way everything feels easier when you are close.”

3. Add a specific memory

Choose one real moment.

Example:

“I keep thinking about that evening when we walked with no plan and somehow it became one of my favorite memories.”

4. Give them something to do next

A small action can make the letter feel comforting.

Example:

“Listen to our song, take a deep breath, and remember that this distance is temporary.”

  • Open when you miss me letter example

You opened this because you miss me.

I wish I could be there with you right now. I wish I could sit next to you, hold your hand, and make this feeling a little softer. But since I cannot, I hope these words can do a small part of that.

I miss you too. I miss the way you look at me when you are trying not to smile. I miss our ordinary moments — the ones that do not sound special when you explain them, but somehow mean everything.

When the distance feels too big, remember that missing each other is not a weakness. It is proof that what we have matters. We are not close every day, but you are still part of my days. You are in the songs I hear, the things I want to tell you, and the future moments I keep imagining.

So for now, breathe. Drink some water. Listen to something that reminds you of us. And please remember this: I love you from here, fully and honestly.

We will see each other again. Until then, keep this letter as a small version of me beside you.

Short version

If you want something shorter, use this:

I know you miss me right now. I miss you too. I wish I could make the distance disappear, but until I can, remember that you are loved, wanted, and held in my heart every day. This distance is hard, but it is not stronger than us.

Prompts to personalize your letter

Before you finish, answer these:

  • What do you miss most about them?
  • What small ordinary moment do you want to remember?
  • What song, place, or object reminds you of them?
  • What do you want them to do after reading?
  • What future moment are you both waiting for?

Add those answers to make the letter feel personal.

Printable or digital idea

You can print this as a physical open when letter and seal it in an envelope. You can also save it as a digital gift capsule that opens on a future date, such as your next visit, anniversary, or reunion.

For long-distance love, combining both formats works well: one printed letter for now, and one future-opening digital capsule for later.

FAQ

What is an open when you miss me letter?
It is a personal letter someone opens when they miss you. It usually includes comfort, love, memories, and reassurance.
What should I write in an open when you miss me letter?
Write that you miss them too, include a shared memory, remind them the distance is temporary, and end with love or reassurance.
Is this good for long-distance relationships?
Yes. An open when you miss me letter is one of the best long-distance relationship letters because it gives your partner something personal to return to when they feel lonely.
