FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a digital time capsule?
A digital time capsule is a collection of memories, thoughts, goals, decisions, photos and messages you save today and reopen in the future. PersonalCapsule lets you seal a capsule and set a date when it unlocks - turning a private note into a conversation with your future self.
Can I write letters to my future self?
Yes. Letters to your future self are the heart of PersonalCapsule. Write a message today, choose when it should reopen, and seal it until that date arrives.
Can I create gift capsules?
Yes. Gift Capsules let you write a meaningful message for someone you love, choose a designed template, and share it as a personal keepsake. They are useful for open when letters, birthdays, anniversaries, long-distance relationships and heartfelt notes.
Does PersonalCapsule require an account?
No. PersonalCapsule does not require sign-up, email or an account. You can start writing in seconds - your capsules belong to you, not to a server account.
Is PersonalCapsule private?
Yes. PersonalCapsule does not collect your capsule text, photos, voice recordings or personal memories. Your private content stays on your device and can sync through Apple iCloud if enabled.
Does it sync with iCloud?
Yes. Your data can sync privately through your personal Apple iCloud account, so your capsules can be available across your devices without passing through PersonalCapsule servers.
Can I add photos?
Yes. Photo attachments are available as a premium feature and can be added to any capsule, so a memory can preserve how it looked, not only what you wrote about it.
Can I add voice recordings?
Yes. Voice recordings are available as a premium feature and can be added to any capsule, so a moment can preserve how it sounded and how you felt while speaking it.
Can I export my data?
Yes. PDF export is available as a premium feature, so you can turn capsule content into a document when you need it.
What happens when a capsule opens?
You choose the reopen date when you create a capsule. Until then it stays sealed; when the date arrives, the capsule unlocks and is ready to read again.
