FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a digital time capsule?
A digital time capsule is a collection of memories, thoughts, goals, decisions, photos and messages you save today and reopen in the future. PersonalCapsule lets you seal a capsule and set a date when it unlocks — turning a private note into a conversation with your future self.
Can I write letters to my future self?
Yes. Letters to your future self are the heart of PersonalCapsule. Write a message today, choose when it should reopen — six months, a year, or further out — and seal it until that date arrives.
Does PersonalCapsule require an account?
No. There's no sign-up, no email and no tracking. You can start writing in seconds — your capsules belong to you, not to a server.
Does it sync with iCloud?
Yes. Your data stays on your device and syncs privately through your personal iCloud, so your capsules are available across your devices without ever passing through our servers.
Can I attach photos and voice notes?
Yes. Alongside written letters you can add photos and voice notes, so a capsule can capture how a moment looked and sounded — not just what you wrote about it.
What can I capture besides letters?
PersonalCapsule supports letters to your future self, memories, goals and life decisions — each as its own capsule type — plus Home Screen widgets that keep your countdowns in view.
When can I reopen a capsule?
You choose the reopen date when you create a capsule. Until then it stays sealed; when the date arrives, the capsule unlocks and is ready to read again.
Is my data private?
Privacy is the foundation of the app. There's no account, your content is processed on-device, and syncing happens only through your own iCloud. You can read more in the privacy policy.
