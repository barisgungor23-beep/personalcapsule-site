Changelog

PersonalCapsule is actively improving.

A short history of meaningful updates to the private digital time capsule journal.

v1.1.0

Photos, voice notes, widgets and Turkish language support

This update expanded PersonalCapsule beyond written capsules and made the app more expressive, visual and useful day to day.

  • Photo attachments for capsules
  • Voice recording for capsules
  • Home Screen widgets for capsule countdowns
  • Locking support for Decision, Goal and Moment capsules
  • Turkish language support with in-app language selection
  • PDF export and offer code redemption
v1.0.0

Initial release

The first release introduced the core idea: create private capsules for your future self and reopen them later.

  • Letters to your future self
  • Decision, Goal and Moment capsule types
  • Reminder dates and reopen flow
  • Private on-device storage with iCloud sync support
  • Lifetime premium upgrade
