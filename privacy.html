Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Overview

PersonalCapsule is built with privacy as a core principle. Your capsules, decisions, goals, memories, and letters are deeply personal — and they stay that way.

Data Collection

PersonalCapsule does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. We do not have accounts, login systems, or cloud infrastructure.

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced via Apple's iCloud service. We have no access to your iCloud data. Only you, through your Apple ID, can access your capsules.

Analytics

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. No data about your usage, behavior, or content is collected or shared with anyone.

Third-Party Services

PersonalCapsule uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and Apple's iCloud for data synchronization. These services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies. No other third-party services are used.

Biometric Data

If you enable Face ID or Touch ID lock, biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on your device. No biometric data is accessed, stored, or transmitted by PersonalCapsule.

Children's Privacy

PersonalCapsule does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
