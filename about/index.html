What is PersonalCapsule?

PersonalCapsule is a private digital time capsule journal for saving letters, memories, goals and decisions for your future self.

Instead of treating journaling as a daily task, PersonalCapsule focuses on meaningful moments. You write something now, choose a future date, and let that capsule wait until the right time to be opened again.

Why it was created

PersonalCapsule was created for people who want to remember not only what happened, but how life felt while it was happening.

Big choices, quiet memories, hopes, fears and goals often make sense only after time passes. The app gives those moments a private place to wait until your future self is ready to meet them again.

Who built it

PersonalCapsule is built in Istanbul by Umut Baris Gungor, an indie iOS developer based in Turkey.

The product is designed as a privacy-first journaling app rather than a large social platform. Its purpose is simple: help people reflect across time without needing an account or public profile.

Privacy approach

PersonalCapsule does not collect your journal content, capsule text, photos, voice recordings or personal memories. Your private content stays on your device and can sync through Apple iCloud if enabled.

The app uses Apple's StoreKit for purchases and Apple's iCloud for optional sync. PersonalCapsule does not run its own account system for your private capsule content.

Free vs Premium
