What is PersonalCapsule?

PersonalCapsule is a private digital time capsule journal for iPhone. It is designed to help you save thoughts, emotions, decisions, goals and memories in a way that becomes more meaningful over time.

You can write a letter to your future self, save an important memory, record a life decision, set a personal goal, or create a Gift Capsule for someone you love.

  • Write future self letters and reopen them later.
  • Create digital time capsules for memories, goals and decisions.
  • Make Gift Capsules and Open When letters for meaningful moments.
  • Reflect privately without public posting, feeds or social pressure.

What are Gift Capsules?

Gift Capsules are private messages, memories, or Open When letters created for someone special. They are meant for moments where a normal message feels too small: birthdays, anniversaries, long-distance relationships, encouragement, family memories, or future messages.

The idea is simple: write something personal, choose when it should be opened, and let the message wait until the right time.

Why it was created

PersonalCapsule was created because ordinary notes often capture what happened, but not why it mattered.

Some thoughts only become clear with distance. A fear that felt huge today may feel small in a year. A decision that felt uncertain may become a turning point. A simple memory may become something you are grateful you saved.

The app gives those moments a quiet place to wait until your future self is ready to meet them again.

Who built it

PersonalCapsule is built by Umut Baris Gungor, an indie iOS developer from Istanbul, Turkey.

The app is not built as a social network, advertising platform, or content-sharing service. It is built as a personal reflection tool for people who want a private, focused place to save meaningful thoughts and return to them later.

Long-term commitment

PersonalCapsule is an actively developed indie app. The goal is to keep improving the app carefully over time, without turning it into a complicated or noisy platform.

  • Updates are planned around privacy, reliability and meaningful reflection.

Privacy approach

PersonalCapsule is designed around a simple privacy idea: your personal capsule content should not become content for a server, feed, or profile.

  • No PersonalCapsule account is required.
  • Your capsule content is designed to stay on your device.
  • Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple iCloud account.
  • PersonalCapsule does not collect your journal text, capsule text, photos, voice recordings or private memories.
  • Purchases are handled through Apple's StoreKit.

Your data is not locked to a PersonalCapsule server

PersonalCapsule does not require you to create a server-based journal account. Your private capsule content is not built around a PersonalCapsule cloud database.

Premium users can export capsules as PDF. This helps you keep important letters, memories, decisions, goals or Gift Capsules as personal archives, printed keepsakes, or files you can store outside the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PersonalCapsule used for?
PersonalCapsule is used to create private digital time capsules on iPhone. You can write future self letters, save memories, record life decisions, track personal goals, and create Gift Capsules or Open When letters for someone you love. It is designed for meaningful reflection over time rather than public posting or daily social journaling.
Do I need an account to use PersonalCapsule?
No. PersonalCapsule does not require a PersonalCapsule account. Your private capsule content is designed to stay on your device. If you choose to use iCloud sync, your data syncs through your own Apple iCloud account.
What is a Gift Capsule?
A Gift Capsule is a private message, memory, or Open When letter created for someone special. You can use it for birthdays, anniversaries, long-distance relationships, family memories, encouragement, or future messages. You write the capsule, choose an unlock date, and let it wait until the right time.
What is included in the free version?
The free version lets you create up to 3 capsules and your first Gift Capsule. You can write future self letters, goals, decisions, and memories, then seal them until a chosen date. Lifetime Premium unlocks unlimited capsules, unlimited Gift Capsules and templates, photo attachments, voice notes, Home Screen widgets, and PDF export.
Can I export my capsules?
Yes. Premium users can export capsules as PDF. PDF export helps you preserve important letters, memories, decisions, goals, or Gift Capsules outside the app. It can be useful for personal archives, printed keepsakes, or saving meaningful capsules in another format.
